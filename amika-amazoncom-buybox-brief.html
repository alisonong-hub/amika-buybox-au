Action

Disable International Shipping
to AU on Amazon.com

Effort Level

This is a Seller Central / Vendor Central settings change — it does not require listing changes, pricing negotiation, or removal of products. It is low-effort, fully reversible, and has zero cost.

The Amika US team managing the amazon.com 1P relationship can disable AU-specific international shipping by following the steps below. This prevents Amazon.com from appearing as a seller on Amazon AU listings.

1
Log into Amazon Seller Central (US account)
Use the account that manages Amika's US 1P vendor/seller relationship on amazon.com.
2
Navigate to Account Settings → Shipping Settings
Go to Settings > Shipping Settings > International Shipping or within individual product/offer settings.
3
Identify Australia in the international shipping destinations
Under international or global shipping templates, locate Australia as a destination country.
4
Remove / disable Australia from eligible ship-to countries
Uncheck or remove "Australia" from the shipping template. This will prevent Amazon.com from offering cross-border fulfillment to AU customers on Amika ASINs.
5
Confirm change and allow 24-48 hours to propagate
Amazon.com should cease appearing as a seller on Amazon AU listings within 1-2 business days of the change being saved.
Note for Vendor Central (1P)

If Amika's US amazon.com relationship is through Vendor Central (not Seller Central), the international shipping restriction may need to be coordinated with the Amazon US vendor manager or category team. The lever is the same, but the pathway is through a vendor contact or case rather than a self-service settings page.
